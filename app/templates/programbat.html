Fruit Bat

Info

The Talaud fruit bat (Acerodon humilis) is a large fruit bat species. It is notable for its limited range and vulnerability to habitat loss. This species is confined to the Talaud Islands, located between northern Sulawesi and Mindanao. Its dependence on primary forest makes it especially sensitive to habitat changes.

Threats

Acerodon humilis is listed as Vulnerable because of its restricted range. Deforestation for agriculture is the primary threat, combined with hunting pressure. Conservation priorities include protecting remaining forest habitats and enforcing hunting restrictions across the Talaud Islands.
