Green Sea Turtle

Info

The green sea turtle (Chelonia mydas) is a large, herbivorous marine reptile found in tropical and subtropical oceans worldwide. It is the only primarily plant-eating species among sea turtles and plays an essential ecological role in maintaining healthy seagrass beds. The species is classified as endangered due to human exploitation and habitat loss.

Threats

Principal threats include entanglement in fishing gear, poaching for meat and eggs, coastal development, pollution, and climate-driven changes to nesting beaches. Global conservation programs are helping populations recover, though declines persist in many regions.
