Our Mission

Protecting Indonesia's biodiversity through digital innovation.

Who We Are

Komodo Hub is a unified digital platform dedicated to the preservation of Indonesia's endangered species. We believe that conservation starts with education and ends with community action. By bridging the gap between researchers, schools, and local communities, we create a powerful network for environmental change.

Education

Providing interactive resources to teach the next generation about wildlife.

Research

Empowering local researchers with open-source tools and data sharing.

Community

Connecting people across Indonesia to take real-world conservation steps.
