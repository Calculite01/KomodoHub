Sumatran Tiger

Info

The Sumatran tiger (Panthera tigris sumatrae) is the smallest and rarest living tiger subspecies, found only on the Indonesian island of Sumatra. Critically endangered, it represents the last surviving population of the Sunda Island tigers. The Sumatran tiger has darker orange fur and more closely spaced black stripes than mainland tigers, a camouflage adaptation to dense jungle environments.

Threats

Rampant deforestation for palm oil, pulp, and acacia plantations, combined with poaching and human–tiger conflict, has devastated their numbers. Illegal wildlife trade remains a major danger—tiger bones, skins, and teeth are sold despite strict national protection laws.
