Saving animals, Education, & Research, Made Fun!

Komodo Hub empowers communities across Indonesia to protect and preserve endangered species through one unified digital platform. By combining education, exploration, and real conservation programs, we make it easy for schools and communities to get involved. Together, we transform awareness into meaningful action for a more sustainable future.
